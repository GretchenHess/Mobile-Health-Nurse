Why It Works

I'm not just a nurse.
I'm proof it's possible.

Eight years as an ICU nurse — including on the frontlines through COVID-19. At 54, I'm on zero medications. I swim, bike, run, lift weights, train for triathlons, hike, and golf. Every week.

I've stood at the bedside of people far younger than me who had lost control of their health. I know what makes the difference. I built this practice to help you get there — whether you're starting from scratch, managing a condition, keeping your parent safe, or ready to train at a higher level.

What I Believe

The principles behind this practice.

Decline Is Not Inevitable

The choices you make today write the story of your health at 70, 80, and beyond. I've seen both outcomes. The difference is largely within your control.

🩺

You Deserve More Than 10 Minutes

Real healthcare happens in conversation. My model is built around time, attention, and genuine clinical expertise — in your home, on your terms.

Prevention Is Power
