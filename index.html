A Different Kind of Healthcare

I'm a nurse. I'm here. Call me when you need one.

Mobile Health Nurse isn't a clinic, an app, or a portal. It's a resource — a real RN you can call, text, or email when something doesn't feel right, when you're scared, when you have questions nobody else has time to answer. Whether you book a visit or just need to think out loud with someone clinical, that's what I'm here for.

Why It Works

I'm not just a nurse.
I'm proof it's possible.

Eight years as an ICU nurse — including on the frontlines through COVID-19. At 54, I'm on zero medications. I swim, bike, run, lift weights, train for triathlons, hike, and golf. Every week.

I've stood at the bedside of people far younger than me who had lost control of their health. I know exactly what makes the difference. And I built this practice to help you get there — whether you're starting from scratch, managing a condition, or trying to keep your parent safe.

What I Believe

The principles behind this practice.

🏃

Decline Is Not Inevitable

The choices you make today write the story of your health at 70, 80, and beyond. I've seen both outcomes. The difference is real, and it's largely within your control.

🩺

You Deserve More Than 10 Minutes

Real healthcare happens in conversation, not in rushed appointments. My model is built around time, attention, and genuine clinical expertise — in your home, on your terms.

🛡️

Prevention Is Power

I'm not here to manage your decline. I'm here to help you protect your HealthSpan — the years of your life lived in full strength, independence, and quality.
