Health Clarity Visit

$250One-Time · In-Home · 90 min

A comprehensive, one-on-one in-home health assessment. I come to you, spend real time with you, and leave you with a personalized written visit summary in plain language you can actually use. This is your baseline — on paper, in your hands, on the day we meet.

The Health Clarity Visit stands completely on its own. Whether or not you continue with me afterward, you'll leave with a clear picture of where your health stands and what to do next.

  • Full vital signs assessment & documentation
  • Head-to-toe physical health review
  • Medication review & interaction check
  • Lab review & plain-language interpretation
  • Health history & current concerns discussion
  • Nutrition overview
  • Functional movement screen
  • Goal-setting conversation
  • Nursing recommendations
  • Written visit summary — yours to keep

Health Partnership

From $325Per Month · In-Home · 75 min · Includes Personal Training

Every visit builds on the last. I already know your baseline, your medications, your goals, and your story. We check your numbers against your own trend line, talk through anything new — doctor visits, confusing test results, medication changes — and I translate it all into plain language and clear direction. Then we train. Every single visit.

Health Partnership includes full access to me between visits. Call or text anytime — you'll hear from me directly, not an automated system.

  • In-home RN visit — 75 min per session
  • Personal training included every visit
  • Vital signs tracking & trending
  • Medication & lab review
  • Doctor visit follow-up & clarification
  • Nutrition & movement coaching
  • Written progress summaries
  • Chronic condition monitoring
  • Full access — call or text me directly
  • Early intervention & care coordination

Nurse Direct Line

$149Per Month · Virtual · Available Nationwide

Direct access to an ICU-trained RN — by call or text, whenever you need it. Available to anyone in Arizona or across the United States. No in-home visit required. No portal, no automated system, no stranger answering your question.

This is the right service if you've had a Health Clarity Visit and aren't ready for Health Partnership, or if you simply want a clinical voice you can reach when something comes up — a confusing lab result, a medication question, a symptom you're not sure about, a doctor's recommendation you don't understand.

When you call

I answer. If I'm with a client, leave a message and I'll call you back within 2 hours.

When you text

I respond personally within 2 hours during waking hours. Late night texts will have a response waiting by morning.

  • Unlimited call & text access
  • ICU-trained RN — not a call center
  • Medication questions & concerns
  • Lab result interpretation
  • Symptom triage & guidance
  • Doctor recommendation clarification
  • Pre-appointment preparation
  • Available anywhere in the U.S.
