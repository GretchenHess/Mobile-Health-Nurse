Medical Advocacy

$200In-Person · As Needed · First 2 hrs
$200 first 2 hours

An ICU-trained nurse with you in the doctor's office, hospital room, or before a procedure. Your care — or your loved one's care — improves when the doctor knows a nurse advocate is part of the care plan.

Most patients leave appointments confused, having nodded along to clinical language they didn't fully understand. I make sure that doesn't happen to you. I prepare you beforehand, translate in real time, and debrief you after so nothing gets lost.

  • Pre-visit question preparation
  • Real-time clinical interpretation
  • Ensure your concerns are fully addressed
  • Medication & treatment plan review
  • Post-visit plain-language debrief
  • Written visit summary — yours to keep
  • Help evaluating options & next steps
  • Hospital room presence & support
  • Pre-procedure preparation
  • Coordination with care team when needed
$200 minimum covers the first two hours. $50 for each additional 30 minutes. Travel within West Valley included. Distance outside Goodyear, Litchfield Park, and Avondale may incur a travel fee — ask when you call.
