What a Session Looks Like

Structured. Specific. Yours.

Every session follows the same intentional framework — with adjustments based on how you feel and what your body needs that day.

1

Movement Screen & Check-In

We start with how you move — not how much you can lift. I assess your patterns, note any changes since last session, and confirm your goals for the day.

2

Corrective Warm-Up

Targeted activation and mobility work woven into your warm-up — not skipped. If something needs addressing, it gets addressed before we load.

3

Progressive, Coached Training

Strength work built around your mechanics and goals. I cue in real time, adjust load when needed, and track what's working so every session builds on the last.

4

Take-Home Plan

You leave every session with specific exercises or cues to reinforce between sessions. Progress isn't reserved for when I'm in the room.
