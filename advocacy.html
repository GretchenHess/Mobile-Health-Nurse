From the inside

What I know from years in the ICU.

I've spent years as an ICU nurse watching patients and families navigate some of the hardest moments of their lives. And I can tell you from experience — having someone who knows the system sitting next to you changes everything.

Not because doctors are bad. Most are excellent. But a busy physician moves fast, uses clinical language, and has twelve more patients waiting. Important things get missed — not out of negligence, but out of pace. When a nurse is in the room, the conversation slows down. Questions get asked. Answers get clarified.

When a physician sees an ICU nurse sitting with their patient, they bring their best.

Most patients and families are non-medical. They nod along, leave confused, and make decisions without ever feeling truly informed. That's not a failure of intelligence — it's a failure of the system. The system moves fast and speaks in a language most people were never taught.

I'll be with you as your advocate, your translator, and someone who genuinely cares about the outcome. You deserve to leave every appointment knowing exactly what happened, what it means, and what comes next.
