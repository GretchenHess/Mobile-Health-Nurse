From Gretchen

I think of this as a neighbor visit.

I've been a nurse for nearly ten years. ICU through COVID. Pre-op and PACU. Now a personal trainer competing in triathlons. I've sat with people at their most uncertain moments, and I've noticed something: most people are carrying a health question they've never gotten around to asking.

That question deserves a real conversation. Not a Google search. Not a patient portal. Just two people and a cup of coffee.

Bring whatever is on your mind. Your health, your fitness, your energy, your concerns. I'll listen and share what I know. No agenda. No clipboard. No pressure to do anything afterward.

I'm so glad you found this.
